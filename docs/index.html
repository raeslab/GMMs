Species–function relationships shape ecological properties of the human gut microbiome

Vieira-Silva S, Falony G, Darzi Y, Lima-Mendez G, Garcia Yunta R, Okuda S, Vandeputte D, Valles-Colomer M, Hildebrand F, Chaffron S, Raes J

Interactive metabolic maps of the gut-specific pathway modules (GMMs). The module map connects modules by their input and output compounds; the hierarchy map gives an overview of the major metabolic processes in the gut.

Metabolic maps

Open the interactive metabolic maps

The module map consists of gut-specific pathway modules connected by their input and output compounds. Modules are clustered and coloured according to their hierarchical classification (e.g. amino-acid degradation); metabolites are circles and modules are coloured lines. The hierarchy map is a high-level map of the major metabolic processes in the gut. Click a module or process to see its details.
